Historical Product Demand — EDA Dashboard

SKU-level order demand across warehouses and product categories, 2011–2017. Interactive companion to the full Jupyter notebook.

Data quality caveats (handled in cleaning, shown for transparency)

  • Missing dates in a meaningful share of rows — excluded from time-series charts, kept for category/product rollups.
  • Exact duplicate rows (~11%) — no order-ID column exists to confirm whether these are real repeat orders or an export artifact, so they are kept, not dropped.
  • Order_Demand was stored as text with parentheses marking negative values (returns/cancellations) — converted to a signed integer before analysis.
